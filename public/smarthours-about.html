The digital opening hours sign for your business

Keep customers informed with SmartHours, a battery-powered digital opening hours sign for your shop window. Sync your hours with Google Business Profile or manage your display from your smartphone or PC. Enjoy long battery life and a tidy installation without power cables during use.

  • Google Business Profile sync Keep shop window hours in sync with Google so customers always see the right open and closed times.
  • Manage from your phone or PC Control your digital opening hours sign from a smartphone or computer, with no extra hardware on the counter.
  • Long battery life Run the display through the working week on battery power, with no power cables during use.
  • Tidy shop-window install Mount on glass without drilling, so the digital sign stays neat in the window.

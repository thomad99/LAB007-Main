USAGE NOTES
White is for dark backgrounds; black/navy are for light backgrounds.
Cyan and teal are alternate brand colours. Check contrast on your destination.
Social sites control their own background and cropping and may flatten transparency.
Larger icons have generous padding to help protect the silhouette in circular crops.
16–64 px icons use tighter padding to remain legible at small sizes.
Tiny clock and handle details inevitably lose definition at 16 px.

App Store: the 1024 px PNG is transparent source artwork as requested. For a
conventional flattened iOS App Store icon, add an opaque background in your app
asset workflow. For Icon Composer, use this as foreground artwork with an
appropriate background layer. Do not treat this PNG pack as a finished .icon
asset, Xcode asset catalogue or app-store submission. Platforms apply their own
icon shapes; no rounded-square background is baked into these files.
Apple touch icons may also need an opaque background for predictable display.

These are raster exports from the approved logo. The original extracted icon is
about 391 x 460 px; the larger PNGs are smoothly upscaled, not vector artwork.

## Welcome

Welcome to Elite Cleaning Services. We take pride in delivering a higher standard of clean for our clients. This short guide covers what we expect from every team member.

---

## Pay

- Employees are paid **weekly**.
- Pay is issued on the same day each week.
- Hours and jobs completed must be reported accurately and on time.

---

## Professional Standards

All employees are expected to:

- Arrive **on time** and ready to work
- Dress **neatly and appropriately** for cleaning work
- Treat **clients, homes, and property** with respect
- Use **quality products** safely and as directed
- Deliver **high attention to detail** on every job
- Communicate clearly and politely with clients and management
- Report problems, damages, or concerns **immediately**
- Keep client information **private and confidential**

---

## Working With Clients

Our clients are an important part of the business. Please represent Elite Cleaning Services professionally at all times and direct all work and payment through the company. Do not offer private cleaning services to clients or share personal contact details for work outside the company.

---

## While You Work for Us

- Follow all job instructions and checklists provided
- Use only approved supplies and equipment
- Do not enter areas or use items not included in the job scope without permission
- Do not share client addresses, schedules, or personal details with anyone outside the company
